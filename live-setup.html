FROM YOUR PORTAL TO YOUR STREAM

One camera. Separate audience outputs.

DigiPoly Live runs on your streaming computer. Environments and uploaded media are downloaded from your private cloud workspace; webcam frames stay local.

Get ready to stream

  1. Configure environments and channel assignments in the online studio.
  2. Click Save to cloud to upload the environment, media and backgrounds.
  3. Open DigiPoly Live, sign in with the same identity, and click Download cloud copy.
  4. Choose your environment and click Load environment.
  5. Start your webcam and place both printed markers in view.
  6. Copy each audience’s OBS link into a separate Browser Source, set to 1280 × 720.

Keep Live running while streaming. Add microphone audio separately in OBS. Each audience source contains a complete video frame.

The initial Mac preview is supplied as a local app build. Public installer distribution will follow signing and notarisation.

Cloud saves are versioned. Downloaded files remain available offline, and changes are applied only when you load an environment. Two sources in OBS do not automatically create two broadcasts or route viewers by country.

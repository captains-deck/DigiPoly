ONE MARKER. MANY WAYS TO USE IT.

Your set. Every market.
Every next idea.

01 / MARKETS & LANGUAGES

One stream setup. Different audiences.

Configure separate video editions with different product advertisements for different markets or languages. Give each audience its own creative, then send the right output through your streaming setup.

02 / BRAND REFRESHES

Refresh your message, not your room.

Update your branding or advertisements digitally without renovating your set. Swap the artwork on a tracked placement when your campaign changes, while the physical scene stays ready to shoot.

03 / SHARED SETS

One set. More than one brand.

Use the same physical set for different brands, sponsors or ads. Assign different media to each campaign channel and create a new branded edition from the same camera view.

04 / MOVING PLACEMENTS

Let the message move with you.

Track a visible marker as an object moves, keeping its digital placement attached in the video. Explore applications on sports clothing or vehicles, with a clear, flat marker in view; fabric deformation, blur and occlusion can affect tracking.

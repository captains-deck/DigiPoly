01 / CREATORS

Your stream, refreshed.

Change sponsor artwork, launch a new look or rotate campaign creative in your streaming scene. Use DigiPoly outputs in a Twitch workflow through your broadcast setup.

02 / CONVERSATIONS

Bring your brand to the call.

Explore branded scenes for video conferencing, webinars and Microsoft Teams. Route the video through a compatible capture or virtual-camera setup.

03 / CAMPAIGNS

Different markets. Same moment.

Prepare English artwork for one audience and Spanish creative for another. Give each channel its own product, sponsor or message from the same source scene.

04 / LIVE EVENTS

A new sponsor. No new set.

Explore audience-specific branding for sports coverage, conferences and international events. Update digital placements without swapping the physical signage.

A LITTLE CLARITY

You choose the editions.

The current prototype creates separate video outputs. Audience and language labels help you organise them; they do not translate artwork, locate viewers or automatically broadcast to Twitch or Teams. Supply the right creative and distribute each feed through your video tools.
